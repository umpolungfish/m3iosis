Sum eigenvalue phases on the signed branch when computing the winding

phases_deg is wrapped into [0, 360) for display, and the Omega mapping summed
that as a total winding. An eigenvalue whose true phase is zero-from-below
(1 - 1e-16j, which is what the identity braid produces) wraps to 359.9999 and
contributes a spurious full turn.

The consequence was that Omega was not a braid invariant and not even a function
of the resulting unitary. sigma_1 and sigma_5 commute, so [1,5,-1,-5] and
[-1,-5,1,5] are the same braid; their unitaries agree to 1e-16 and both are the
identity, every other slot of the tuple matches, and Omega came out ah for one
and awe for the other. [1,3,-1,-3] scored five turns and read as non-Abelian.

It also put a false trend in the strand sweep, since a larger fusion space
offers more eigenvalues to wrap: Omega appeared to climb its constructor order
along the Fibonacci dimensions when it was counting float sign dust.

A winding is signed and must be summed unwrapped. On the (-180, 180] branch
every spelling of the identity gives the trivial class.
