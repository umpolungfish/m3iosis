The anyons evaluate the Jones polynomial rather than trace it.

What stood in jones_polynomial was a normalized trace: no writhe correction,
no weighting by quantum dimension, and so not the invariant. The quantum trace
runs over both total-charge sectors, tau^n -> 1 weighted by one and tau^n -> tau
weighted by phi, the latter realized as the vacuum sector on one extra strand
with the last one left alone.

The two normalization constants are forced, not fitted. Requiring the unknot to
evaluate to 1 in its three Markov presentations is two constraints in two
unknowns, and it returns the framing phase e^{-i pi/5} and the loop value -phi,
which are the constants the theory names. Every knot after that is a prediction:
the trefoil, its mirror, the figure-eight and the cinquefoil all land on their
exact values.

sigma_1 here is the negative crossing in the standard Jones orientation, so the
word is mirrored inside the function. That showed up as the trefoil pair
swapping cleanly while the amphichiral and real-valued knots passed untouched,
which is what identified it as a convention rather than an error.

Chirality is detected exactly when the value has nonzero imaginary part, and
that is forced: integer coefficients plus t^-1 = conj(t) on the unit circle give
V(K*) = conj(V(K)). One root is not a complete invariant, and 8_19 shows why,
since t^3 + t^5 - t^8 collapses to 1 under t^5 = 1.
