Flag a degenerate fusion space instead of reporting invariants off it

fusion_space_dimension is dim Hom(tau^n, 1), the vacuum sector, F_{n-1}. At
three strands that is one-dimensional, so the braid representation is 1x1 and
||[sigma_1, sigma_2]|| is exactly 0.000000 — every non-Abelian invariant the
bridge reports for a 3-strand word is a property of a trivial representation,
not of Fibonacci anyons. Nothing downstream could tell the difference, and a
sweep of 5460 three-strand words produced a whole Omega census off those 1x1
matrices.

The familiar B_3 statement lives in the other sector. Hom(tau^3, tau) is
2-dimensional, which is where sigma_1 sigma_2 sigma_1 |tau> = a|1tau> + b|tau
tau> and the commutator norm 1.799 come from. The sectors interleave —
Hom(tau^n, tau) is F_n and so matches Hom(tau^{n+1}, 1) — and four strands here
reproduces 1.798907 exactly.

A fusion space below dimension two now raises a RuntimeWarning naming the sector
and pointing at n >= 4. Behaviour is otherwise unchanged: the conclusion that
Omega never reaches the non-Abelian class across the swept range stands, because
the vacuum sweep at n=4..8 already covers the tau sector at n=3..7.
